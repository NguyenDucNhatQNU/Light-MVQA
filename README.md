## Abstract

<!--
Medical visual question answering (Med-VQA) is a crucial task in medical AI systems that generate answers from medical images and related queries to support clinical decision making and patient care. In real application scenarios, deploying these systems requires not only performance but also computational efficiency. Large vision-language models (VLMs) based approaches raise significant challenges in terms of computational costs, inference latency, and hallucinations. This paper proposes a novel approach that utilizes a small VLM integrated with multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for Med-VQA. We employ a lightweight model to maintain the robust performance of larger models while consuming fewer resources and minimizing computational time. Additionally, as we utilize multimodal RAG to generate answers that closely align with specialized expertise and minimizes hallucinations. Our experiments on two benchmark datasets indicate the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed method compared with baselines. Particularly, our approach achieves competitive performance to larger VLM-based baseline, while requiring significantly shorter inference times and lower hardware cost. These results demonstrate the potential of small VLMs with multimodal RAG for Med-VQA in real-world environments, especially in edge computing and low-resource settings. More potentially, it may influence the emergence of low-cost, lightweight, and deployable medical AI systems. -->
